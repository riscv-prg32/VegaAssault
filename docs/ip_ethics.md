# Intellectual Property, Ethics, and Fan-Work Scope

This repository is an unofficial educational fan project inspired by the fictional world of *UFO Robot Grendizer*. The MIT license applies only to original code and original project assets contributed here. It cannot grant rights in third-party characters, names, designs, trademarks, animation, manga, or music.

For publication, maintainers should clearly identify the work as unofficial and non-commercial, avoid implying endorsement, and remove any asset whose provenance cannot be documented. The project deliberately uses original pixel art and original chiptune/SFX rather than extracted production material. A public CartridgeStore operator remains responsible for assessing the legal requirements applicable to that deployment and jurisdiction.

The game is fan made with no business target, just a tribute. AGENTS.md requires cartridge compliance and prohibits infringing material. The revised soundtrack uses newly authored note patterns with the existing original waveform samples; no recordings, transcriptions, or third-party musical assets were imported. Non-commercial intent does not grant permission to copy protected material.

The sprite refinement uses locally authored polygon geometry and a project-specific RGB565 palette. No reference images, scans, downloaded sprites, logos or model sheets were used. Dark contours, stepped highlights and articulated armor borrow general mecha drawing conventions; the split crest, chest panels, docking wings and enemy silhouettes are composed in the generator. This documents asset provenance, not a grant of rights in the underlying franchise.

The 2026-09-03 music revision was authored from scratch using simple harmonic synthesis and new note arrays. Its connection to classic heroic anime music is limited to general instrumentation, march-like accents and call-and-response phrasing. No theme recording, score, transcription, sampled instrument or recognizable theme hook was used as input. This records the composition process and does not constitute legal clearance or a claim of rights in the Grendizer theme.

The subsequent opening-theme refinement also uses freshly authored notes. Similarity is pursued through minor-key drama, repeated vocal-like calls, brass attacks and bass pickups. No source-theme melody or rhythm was transcribed or used as a template; the changes do not claim legal clearance.
